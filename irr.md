# Index of Relative Rurality (IRR)

The **Index of Relative Rurality (IRR)** is a continuous measure of rurality for U.S. counties, bounded between 0 (most urban) and 1 (most rural). Unlike traditional binary or categorical classifications, the IRR captures fine-grained spatial and demographic variations in rurality across time based on four dimensions: population size, population density, remoteness, and built-up area.
